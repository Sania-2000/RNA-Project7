## Verification of the 50-to-45 RNA Selection

Comparison of the saved 50-RNA dataset with the 45-RNA sensitivity datasets confirms four common exclusions: `3egz_B`, `3p22_E`, `6t2c_V`, and `8hzj_A`.

Each sensitivity scenario additionally excludes one of two identical-sequence records with differing structural annotations: `7r6q_6` or `7u0h_6`.

The exclusion identifiers are reproducible from the saved datasets and calibration script. However, the individual scientific rationale for each of the four common exclusions has not yet been recovered from the available documentation.

These exclusions are therefore reported as exploratory sensitivity filtering, without claiming that every excluded RNA was demonstrably contaminated or incorrectly annotated.
